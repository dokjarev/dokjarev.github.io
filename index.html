Personal learning journal

My language-learning strategy

I practise seven languages by focusing on one language each week, rotating between languages I know and languages I am learning. During each week, I try to read, listen, and answer questions in that language.

Languages in my rotation

  • French
  • Dutch
  • German
  • Spanish
  • English
  • Chinese
  • Italian

Current focus (15 October 2026): English

My first weeks were Italian, then French, then English. The order of the remaining languages is not fixed here.

My weekly approach

  1. Read: deliberately choose reading material in the week's focus language.
  2. Listen: watch or listen to content in that language.
  3. Retrieve and respond: use NotebookLM to generate questions from material I am interested in learning, and answer them in the focus language.

The goal is to combine language immersion with active recall, using subjects I genuinely want to understand. This page documents my current approach, which I may adjust as I learn.
