An initiative in development

Advancing anesthetic care in Colombia

Every patient should receive safe, appropriate anesthetic care, wherever they live.

I am developing an initiative to help advance equitable anesthetic and perioperative care in Colombia, with particular attention to patients in rural areas and communities outside the major cities.

My aim is to build an independent institution that investigates barriers to good care, makes the evidence accessible, and helps translate findings into improvements in practice and public policy. Supporting the professionals who deliver care is an essential part of that work.

Investigate

Study geographic inequalities and barriers involving staffing, equipment, referral pathways, working conditions, and access to continuing professional development.

Publish

Share findings through scientific publications, public reports, and policy briefs that make problems and possible solutions understandable.

Advocate and evaluate

Work with patients, regional clinicians, hospitals, professional societies, and authorities to develop recommendations and assess their effect on care.

Initial focus: continuing professional development

One proposed research priority is how Colombia supports and verifies continued professional competence. Questions include access to relevant education, protected learning time, costs, institutional responsibilities, and the role of continuing education or recertification requirements.

The aim is to establish what helps clinicians maintain current, safe practice and improves patient care. Recommendations should be informed by evidence and by the realities of practising outside major academic centres.

About me

Jimmy Arévalo Romero

I am a Colombian-trained anesthesiologist and physician-scientist based in the Netherlands. I am currently a PhD candidate in the Department of Anesthesiology at Leiden University Medical Center (LUMC).

My professional interests include cardiovascular and thoracic anesthesia, perioperative physiology, clinical pharmacology, and research that helps improve patient care. Through Jarev, I share what I am learning and projects I am developing.
