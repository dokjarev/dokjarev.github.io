# Jarev Academy

Public educational section of [jarev.nl](https://jarev.nl/academy/).

## Current files
- `index.html`: Academy hub, topic tracks, and future media formats.
- `peer-review.html`: Initial peer-review learning module, emphasizing Colombian Journal of Anesthesiology policies.
- `cardiac-hemostasis.html`: Learning roadmap and primary guideline references for cardiac hemostasis.
- `ecg-essentials.html`: **Part I — How clinicians reason with ECGs**. Clinical problem representation, pattern recognition vs hypothesis-driven reasoning, educational evidence, risk of anchoring, systematic verification and technical illustrations. Sources are linked inline at each claim.
- `ecg-clinical-cases.html`: **Part II — ECG reasoning in clinical practice**. Four original progressive clinical examples (chest pain, sudden collapse, declamping, separation from CPB), with diagnostic prompts and linked primary guidelines and literature.
- `styles.css`: Shared Academy styling.

## Content model
Build **one source lesson per topic** first. Adapt that lesson into:
1. A long-form article with sources and a last-reviewed date.
2. A narrated YouTube video or slide presentation.
3. A LinkedIn summary that links back to the article.
4. An Instagram carousel / short explainer.
5. An X thread summarizing one well-defined question.
6. Optional fictional/de-identified educational cases and pointers to licensed or publicly available manuscripts.

Mark planned content as planned until it has actually been published. Cite primary sources and keep a distinction between study notes, guideline summaries, and formal clinical recommendations.

## Suggested additions
- A new educational page for each lesson, linked from its parent track.
- A version or "last reviewed" note and a primary-reference section.
- Separate metadata for evidence certainty and patient population, particularly when distinguishing adult from pediatric practice.
- In future, an index/catalogue for filtering by topic and media format.

## Publication and ethics safeguards
- **Confidential reviews:** never publish unpublished review material, manuscript files, author identities or editor correspondence.
- **Patient cases:** use fictional or appropriately consented/de-identified cases according to applicable institutional rules.
- **Published papers:** link to the publisher DOI / permitted open-access full text; do not repost PDFs or adapted figures without verifying the license or obtaining permission.
- **Guidelines:** paraphrase and cite, and respect figures/tables licensing; distinguish educational interpretation from formal institutional protocols.
- **AI:** journal reviewer policies and ICMJE guidance on confidentiality and permission take precedence over convenience.
- **Identity:** the Academy is independent and is not represented as an official project of the Colombian Journal of Anesthesiology, EACTS, STS, or an employer.

## Maintenance
When updating a topic, check hyperlinks, guidance dates, and any new evidence. Keep the public pages self-contained and easy to view on mobile.

## ECG two-part series — editorial approach (28 October 2026)

- Content is original and independent of institutional teaching materials; do not reuse local university questions, answers, illustrations, or teaching scripts.
- Part I uses Gupta 2024, Ng 2024, Jay 2024, Viljoen 2021, Oh 2022, Nunes de Alencar 2024, and the 2012 ED-history bias study, all cited in context.
- Part II uses 2025 ACC/AHA ACS, 2023 ESC ACS, 2025 AHA ALS, and the 2024 EACTS/EACTAIC/EBCP CPB guideline, as well as the ECG-education papers.
- The scenario prompts are author-generated learning illustrations; the papers are cited for the specific statements they support, not as if they validated the whole teaching sequence.
- Before future finalization: author review of clinical text, references and device/browser display; consider independently created or appropriately licensed ECG tracings in the next iteration.
